### 🎯 Research Interest
Passionate about integrating public single-cell transcriptomic datasets of stromal cells (especially fibroblasts and endothelial cells) to construct robust aging clocks at the sample level. Focus on deciphering aging-related molecular features, identifying key genes/pathways underlying stromal cell aging, and exploring potential therapeutic targets for aging-related diseases and cancer.
