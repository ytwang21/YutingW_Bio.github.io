### 📊 Current PhD Project
#### Construction of Sample-Level Aging Clock Based on Single-Cell Transcriptomics
- **Objective**: Construct a robust aging clock to predict individual age (not tissue age) by integrating public single-cell transcriptomic datasets of stromal cells (fibroblasts and endothelial cells).
- **Methods**: Single-cell data integration, feature selection, machine learning modeling (random forest, deep learning), pathway enrichment analysis.
- **Progress**: Developing the model and validating with multi-tissue datasets; exploring key aging-related genes and pathways.
